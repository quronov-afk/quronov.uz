Dilmurod Quronov Awarded the Commemorative Badge “30 Years of Independence of the Republic of Uzbekistan”

By the relevant decree of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan, Dilmurod Quronov, Professor at Andijan State University and Doctor of Philological Sciences, has been awarded the commemorative badge “30 Years of Independence of the Republic of Uzbekistan”. The scholar has been engaged in research and teaching for 35 years. Dilmurod Quronov Biography English translation

Dilmurod Quronov: Biography

Born in 1960 in Osh Region. In 1987 he graduated with honours from the Andijan State Pedagogical Institute of Languages, qualifying as a teacher of Russian language and literature and of native (Uzbek) language and literature.
